Motor patterns, not spellcheck

ButterKeys watches for the mistakes you repeat — adjacent swaps, early spaces, the classic teh — and only acts when it’s confident.

∿

Melt, don’t nag

High-confidence slips become the right word as you type. Unsure? It leaves your text alone.

⌘

Stays in the bar

Menu bar agent. Undo with ⌃⌥Z. Pause anytime. Code editors stay on a narrower “code-safe” mode.

⌂

Fridge-local

No cloud AI. No sentence storage. Just compact pairs like soem→some if you enable learning.

Your typing stays on your Mac.

Corrections never leave the machine. Update checks (optional) only hit the release feed — never your keystrokes.
